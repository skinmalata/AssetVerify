About Us

Independent verification you can rely on

AssetVerify Property Services helps buyers reduce the risk of fraud, disputed ownership, fake documentation and undisclosed defects before they commit money to property and other valuable assets.

Our mission

To make independent, honest due diligence accessible to everyday buyers in Nigeria — starting in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, and expanding across the country.

We are a private, independent business. We are not a government agency, we do not issue official certificates, and we do not claim legal authority. Where formal legal advice or registered survey work is needed, we coordinate with appropriately qualified professionals.

What we do

  • Review the ownership and identity documents you provide.
  • Coordinate relevant title, registry and survey checks where accessible.
  • Visit and inspect the asset, documenting visible condition and discrepancies.
  • Arrange specialist inspections for buildings, vehicles and machinery.
  • Produce a clear written report of checks, findings and unresolved issues.

Our principles

Honesty over reassurance

We report what we find, including limitations. We will never claim a property is "safe" or "genuine".

Confidentiality

Your documents and personal information are handled securely and shared only as necessary to do the work.

Clarity

We explain what was checked, what was found, and what remains uncertain.

Independence

We do not take commissions from sellers or agents. Our duty is to you, the buyer.

Coverage

Currently Port Harcourt and Rivers State, with expansion planned across Nigeria. We will always tell you honestly whether we can serve a location.
