Why verify

Benefits of independent verification

  • Reduce fraud risk. Fake documents and impersonation are common. Independent checks help reveal red flags early.
  • Surface disputed ownership. Conflicting claims are easier to address before money changes hands.
  • Reveal hidden defects. Physical inspection documents visible condition and defects.
  • Make informed decisions. A written report gives you evidence for negotiations or to walk away.
  • Documentation for your records. Keep a dated record of what was checked and when.

Verification reduces risk but cannot eliminate it. Always consider independent legal advice before completing a purchase.
