1. Who we are

AssetVerify Property Services ("we", "us", "our") is a private, independent property and asset verification business based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. We are the data controller for personal data we collect through this website and in the course of providing our services.

Contact:

2. Information we collect

  • Identity and contact data: your name, email address, phone number, country of residence and preferred contact method.
  • Request data: details about the asset you want verified, including location, seller/agent contact information, asking price and intended purchase date.
  • Documents you upload: survey plans, title documents, receipts, vehicle documents, equipment records and similar files.
  • Account data: if you register, your login credentials (passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see your password).
  • Usage and technical data: limited information required for security, such as timestamps and audit records of actions on your request.

3. Why we process your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotations.
  • To carry out the verification services you request.
  • To coordinate with qualified professionals and third parties where necessary to complete checks.
  • To communicate with you about your request and deliver reports.
  • To maintain records, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We rely on your consent (for example, for document processing), the performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating securely, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. How we store and protect your data

Uploaded documents are stored in private, access-controlled storage. They are not publicly accessible. We share them only with authorised personnel and, where needed for a check, with the relevant qualified professional, using short-lived secure links. Access is restricted by role-based permissions and database-level security policies.

We apply measures including encryption in transit, access controls, audit logging, input validation and least-privilege access. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Who we share it with

  • Qualified professionals (for example surveyors or inspectors) strictly to the extent needed for your verification.
  • Service providers that host our systems (for example our database, storage and email providers), under appropriate terms.
  • Authorities where we are legally required to do so.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep request records and documents for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain business records and meet legal or accounting obligations. When they are no longer needed, we delete or anonymise them. You may request deletion earlier, subject to our legal obligations, by contacting us.

7. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have rights including to be informed, to access your data, to request correction, to request deletion, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details above.

8. Cookies and local storage

This site uses browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. It does not use advertising trackers. Where analytics are added in future, we will update this policy.

9. Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
