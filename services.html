Our Services

Verification services

Each service below combines document review, coordination of searches and inspections, and a written report. We clearly distinguish physical inspection from documentary review and from formal legal verification.

Important: We are not a law firm or a survey company. Legal advice is provided only by appropriately qualified legal practitioners, and technical survey work only by appropriately registered surveyors. Where those are required, we coordinate and clearly label their role.

🗺️ Land Verification

For plots, land and undeveloped property. We help you understand what the documents show, what the records indicate where accessible, and what is physically on the ground.

  • Review available ownership documents.
  • Coordinate title and land registry searches.
  • Coordinate survey-plan checks and charting with qualified surveyors.
  • Investigate relevant government acquisition and encumbrance records where accessible.
  • Conduct a site visit.
  • Document visible boundaries, access, occupation and discrepancies.
  • Provide a report of completed checks and unresolved risks.
Request land verification

Typical documents to prepare

  • Survey plan
  • Title documents / deed / receipt
  • Seller or agent identification and authority
  • Any previous searches or receipts

Coverage and timelines depend on location and record accessibility.

🏠 House & Building Verification

For completed homes, buildings and structures. We review ownership and seller authority, coordinate title searches and inspect visible condition.

  • Review available ownership documents and seller authority.
  • Coordinate relevant title searches.
  • Inspect the property and visible condition.
  • Record occupation, tenancy, access and visible defects.
  • Offer specialist building inspections where needed (third-party fee applies).
Request house verification

Note on building condition

A detailed structural or building-condition inspection is carried out by a specialist and charged separately. Our standard inspection documents visible condition only.

We do not provide architectural, structural or engineering certification.

🚗 Vehicle Verification

For cars, buses, trucks and other vehicles. We check identity and available records, and coordinate mechanical inspection.

  • Check vehicle identity and available registration information.
  • Inspect VIN and chassis identifiers.
  • Review available ownership documentation.
  • Coordinate mechanical inspection and available history checks.
Request vehicle verification

Good to know

History checks depend on what data sources are accessible for the vehicle. We will tell you which checks were possible and which were not.

⚙️ Equipment & Machinery Verification

For generators, industrial machines, construction equipment and other machinery.

  • Check serial numbers and identifying marks.
  • Review proof of ownership and available records.
  • Arrange technical inspection.
  • Document condition and discrepancies.
Request equipment verification

Technical inspections
