Verification Limitations & Disclaimers

Please read this page carefully. It explains what our verification can and cannot do.

We do not guarantee outcomes

Our verification is an independent due-diligence exercise. It is not a guarantee of ownership, title, authenticity, condition or value, and it does not prove that fraud or hidden defects do not exist. A favourable risk category means only that we identified lower risk based on the specific checks we completed.

No government affiliation

We are a private business. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any government agency, land registry, or authority. We do not issue official certificates.

Not legal advice

We do not provide legal advice or legal opinions. Any legal questions about ownership, title or rights must be directed to an appropriately qualified legal practitioner. Where required, we can coordinate with one, but their work is their own.

Survey and technical work

Survey-plan verification and charting are carried out by appropriately registered surveyors. Building, mechanical and technical inspections are carried out by suitably qualified professionals. We coordinate these services; we do not ourselves perform regulated survey or engineering work.

Dependence on third parties and records

  • Registry and government records may be incomplete, unavailable, delayed or inaccurate.
  • Third-party professionals may have their own timelines and limitations.
  • Findings depend on the documents supplied and the access granted. Incomplete documents or denied access limit what we can confirm.
  • Some risks are simply not discoverable through the checks available.

Remote and diaspora clients

Where a client cannot attend in person, verification relies on documents, records and our on-the-ground inspection. This introduces additional limitations, which are recorded in the report.

Risk categories are not conclusions of safety

Our four categories — Lower identified risk, Caution, High concern, and Inconclusive — describe identified risk based on completed checks. They are not certifications of ownership or safety and must not be represented as such.

Your decision remains yours

A verification report is one input among many. Before completing any purchase, obtain independent legal advice and satisfy yourself as to the asset.
