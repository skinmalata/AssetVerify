How It Works

From request to report

A clear, transparent process. You always know the status of your request, what has been checked, and what remains unresolved.

Submit your request

Complete the multi-step form: your details, the asset, the checks you want, and any documents you have. You receive a unique reference number immediately.

Review & scope

We review the request, confirm the scope and provide a quotation. Professional and third-party fees are shown separately from our base fee.

Documents & payment

We tell you exactly what is needed and how to pay by bank transfer, or generate an invoice. We confirm payment manually and by receipt.

Verification begins

We review documents, coordinate search coordination and inspection visits, and document evidence as we go. You can follow progress.

Findings & quality review

Field and desk findings are compiled and reviewed for quality. Unresolved issues and limitations are recorded honestly.

Report delivered

You receive a written, print-friendly report with findings, risk category, limitations and recommended next steps in your secure portal.

What to expect

Timeline

Estimates are given in your quotation. Simple checks may take days; comprehensive land verification can take two to three weeks depending on third parties.

Communication

We update your request at key stages and contact you if we need more information or documents.

Evidence

Where practical we record dated photographs and note the source of each finding, so you can see how conclusions were reached.

Honest about limits

Records can be incomplete, officials may not respond, and some risks are not discoverable. Every report includes a limitations section. Verification reduces risk; it cannot eliminate it or guarantee ownership.
